The Science of Nilotica Shea

Our shea is Vitellaria nilotica — a species native to the Nile basin and prized by formulators for its unique composition. While traditional knowledge of our grandmothers has always known its power, modern science now confirms why it's superior.

Key Characteristics

  • Naturally soft at room temperature (melting point 26-32°C)
  • High oleic acid (50-65%) for faster skin absorption
  • Rich in Vitamin E (tocopherols) — powerful antioxidants
  • High unsaponifiable matter (17%+) for anti-inflammatory properties
