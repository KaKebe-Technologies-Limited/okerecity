The Vision Behind Okere City

In 2019, Okere Parish in Otuke District was a community grappling with the wreckage of the LRA insurgency. The nearest primary school and health center were 10km away. Basic necessities like clean water were scarce.

Donnas Ojok-Okello, an Economics graduate, returned to his ancestral home of Okere Mom-Kok ("a baby should not cry") with a vision: to transform this area into a green, sustainable town driven by its own community.

Today, Okere City is a beehive of activity. Children learn their alphabet, men construct new buildings, and women process shea butter to the rhythm of traditional songs passed down through generations.

"This is a community that has taken ownership of its own growth, putting education, health, social enterprises, and the protection of the shea tree at its center."
