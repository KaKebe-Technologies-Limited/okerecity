Okere City

A Community That Built Itself

In 2019, Okere Parish in Otuke District began a quiet revolution. With no school, no clinic, and no clean water nearby, one man returned home with a plan.

Donnas Ojok-Okello turned ancestral shea trees into an engine for a sustainable rural city — one that today exports to global markets.

"A community that has taken ownership of its own growth."
